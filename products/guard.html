Guard — security clarity without alert overload.

A product direction for connecting identity, cloud, code, secrets, and exposure into a smaller set of understandable security priorities.

DIRECTION

From findings to consequences.

Guard is intended to help teams see which combinations of access, exposure, and configuration create meaningful paths — rather than treating every isolated finding as equal.

01

Connect

Bring identity, cloud, application, and secret context into one security graph.

02

Prioritize

Focus on combinations that create reachable and material risk.

03

Remediate

Explain what to fix first and why the change reduces the path.
