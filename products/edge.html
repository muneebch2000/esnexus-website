PRODUCT / 04 · LONG-TERM RESEARCH

Edge — trust for intelligent machines.

Long-term research into identity, authorization, policy, telemetry, and human oversight when AI systems can influence physical devices and environments.

WHY EDGE

Software is crossing into the physical world.

Robotics, IoT, autonomous systems, and AI-controlled equipment require stronger boundaries around who or what may act, under which policy, and with what human oversight.

Edge is not positioned as a near-term commercial product. It is a research direction for understanding the trust layer intelligent machines will need as autonomy increases.
