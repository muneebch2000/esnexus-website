Twin — consequence intelligence for infrastructure.

Model how identity, cloud resources, network exposure, configuration, and data dependencies connect — then understand the likely blast radius of a planned change before production.

Discuss a pilot ↗All products
THE PROBLEM

Changes are easy. Consequences are not.

Infrastructure teams can see individual resources and plans, but the relationships between permissions, exposure, dependencies, and downstream services are harder to reason about. Twin is designed to make those relationships visible before a risky change is applied.
