RESEARCH

Questions worth building around.

ESNEXUS research explores system behavior, security boundaries, AI autonomy, and the infrastructure around intelligent software. Research is used to pressure-test product ideas and technical assumptions.

01

AI-agent security

How should identity, authorization, approvals, memory, tool access, and data boundaries work when software can act autonomously?

02

System intelligence

How can teams model dependencies and consequences across cloud, identity, network, application, and data layers before making a change?

03

Attack & consequence graphs

Can security findings be transformed into understandable paths that show what an attacker could actually reach and why?

04

RAG & retrieval boundaries

How do retrieval systems prevent cross-tenant exposure, poisoning, untrusted context, and unsafe downstream actions?

05

Physical AI & edge trust

What identity, policy, safety, and human-control layers are required when intelligent software interacts with physical equipment?

RESEARCH METHOD

Research should become evidence.

We use prototypes, adversarial testing, technical experiments, operator interviews, and architecture analysis to decide whether a problem is important enough to become a product.

The goal is not academic volume. The goal is a sharper understanding of where intelligent systems fail, where operators lack visibility, and where a focused product can create leverage.
